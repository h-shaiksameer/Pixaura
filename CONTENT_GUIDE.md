# Pixaura content and advertising guidance

Use this file when creating public copy, ad campaigns, landing pages, or social posts.

## Core message

Pixaura is a clear image utility for converting formats, compressing files, resizing images, and meeting practical file-size requirements.

## Approved product claims

- Converts supported image formats with real server-side encoding.
- Supports pixel and millimeter dimensions with DPI selection.
- Supports batch processing and ZIP downloads.
- Shows output dimensions and output file size.
- Processes uploads for the request without building a permanent image library.

## Avoid unsupported claims

Do not claim unlimited processing, guaranteed quality, permanent privacy guarantees, instant processing for every file, or universal compatibility. Keep file limits and deployment limits transparent.

## Suggested ad copy

**Short:** Convert, compress, and resize images with clear controls and precise results.

**Medium:** Need a smaller JPG, a different image format, or exact millimeter dimensions? Pixaura makes everyday image preparation fast and straightforward.

**Call to action:** Upload an image and get the result you need.

## Launch details to update

Change the site URL, public brand name, support email, analytics ID, and advertising ID in `.env.local` locally or in the hosting provider's environment-variable settings. Update the legal pages with the final business and contact details before publishing ads.
